Cardinal, Fixed and Mutable ✨

The three modalities: how each sign starts, holds and changes.

What are the modalities?

Besides the four elements, astrology sorts the twelve signs into three groups called modalities, or qualities. Each group describes how a sign handles change: whether it starts things, keeps them going, or adapts them. Every modality contains four signs, one from each element, which is why each group feels so varied.

🚀 Cardinal: the starters

Signs: Aries, Cancer, Libra, Capricorn.

Cardinal signs begin each of the four seasons, and they bring the same energy to life: they start things. They are ambitious, proactive and good at taking the first step. They like to lead, set goals and get a project moving. The challenge is staying with something after the excitement of starting fades.

🪨 Fixed: the keepers

Signs: Taurus, Leo, Scorpio, Aquarius.

Fixed signs fall in the middle of each season, when things are at their fullest and most settled. They are steady, loyal and determined. Once they decide something, they stick with it, which makes them dependable and persistent. The challenge is resisting change, and sometimes holding on to a plan or feeling longer than is helpful.

🌊 Mutable: the adapters

Signs: Gemini, Virgo, Sagittarius, Pisces.

Mutable signs come at the end of each season, when the world is preparing to change. They are flexible, curious and quick to adjust. They handle surprises well and are good at seeing several sides of a situation. The challenge is staying focused and knowing when to commit.

How to find your full combination

Each sign has one element and one modality, and the pair is unique. Taurus, for example, is earth and fixed, which makes it very steady and dependable. Gemini is air and mutable, which makes it curious and adaptable. Read our guide to the four elements to see how the two fit together.

How modalities affect relationships

Two cardinal signs may both want to lead, which can bring energy or conflict. Two fixed signs can be deeply loyal but stubborn. Two mutable signs adapt to each other easily but may struggle to decide. Mixing modalities often balances things out: a cardinal sign starts, a fixed sign sustains, and a mutable sign adjusts. A gentle reminder

Modalities are a simple lens on personality, not a rulebook. People can be flexible in one part of life and firm in another. Use these ideas as a conversation starter about how you and the people around you handle change.
